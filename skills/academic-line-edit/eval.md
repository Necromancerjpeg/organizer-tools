# Academic line edit — self-check

Run this after an edit, before returning anything. Answer each check pass or fail. Any fail means fix the draft and run the checks again.

For detect requests, run only the Detect mode section.

## Hard Limits

1. Does the edited draft contain **no** name, number, date, mechanism, example, source, citation, or page reference that was absent from the original?
2. Was every vague claim left intact and flagged, rather than replaced with a specific one?
3. Is every hedge as weak as the author left it, with no claim strengthened?
4. Is all quoted, block-quoted, translated, and cited material byte-identical to the original?
5. Are glossary-controlled and technical terms unchanged and unvaried?
6. Does the edit stay inside paragraphs, with any structural change proposed in **What changed** rather than performed?
7. Is every unsourced claim flagged `[SOURCE NEEDED]` rather than cut or sourced?

## Register

1. Is the active profile stated in the output?
2. **Scholarly and Instructional:** is the conclusion or closing section still present and still doing its work?
3. Is signposting ("this chapter argues," "in what follows") preserved rather than cut as throat-clearing?
4. Are evidential qualifiers preserved, with only genuinely empty ones removed?
5. Are long sentences that carry real conditional or qualifying structure still intact?
6. Is the passive voice retained where the agent is genuinely unknown or irrelevant, and removed elsewhere?
7. Does the draft use the same first-person convention it started with?
8. Do the words restored to ordinary use — foster, facilitate, intricate, multifaceted, meticulous, paramount, realm — survive where they carry literal meaning?

## Voice

1. Does the edit preserve the author's vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humour, uncertainty, digressions, and level of polish?
2. Are strong sentences left alone rather than rewritten for consistency?
3. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no compression that strips character?
4. Would the author recognise the edited draft as their own?

## Patterns

1. Are banned promotional words and genuinely empty phrases removed, except where quoted as examples?
2. Are binary contrasts, negative listings, rhetorical setups, and throat-clearing openers removed — while genuine contrastive definitions survive?
3. Are faux-insight setups, colon reveals, superficial `-ing` analysis, fake-strong verbs, and dramatic fragments fixed?
4. Is synonym cycling collapsed to one consistent term?
5. Is importance puffery replaced with the plain fact already in the draft, and not with an invented one?
6. Are fake-profound kickers deleted rather than improved — except a content-carrying closing image in Public profile?
7. Is formatting slop removed: emoji headings, decorative bold, bullets that should be prose, headings over tiny sections?
8. Are em dashes no more frequent than the author's own rate, with no clusters?

## Final read

1. Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked punchy fragments — the standard overcorrection?
2. Would the edited draft sound natural read aloud to a colleague in the field?
3. Does the output include the full edited draft, a **What changed** section, and an **Author work required** list?

## Detect mode

1. Is every finding named as a pattern from this skill, with the line quoted and a short fix?
2. Is the draft left entirely unrewritten?
3. Is there no score, ranking, percentage, or claim about whether AI wrote the text?
4. For student work, is the mode detect and only detect?
